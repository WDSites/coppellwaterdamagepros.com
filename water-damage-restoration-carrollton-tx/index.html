Water damage restoration in Carrollton, TX is a job shaped by when the city was built. Carrollton grew from 13,855 residents in 1970 to 40,595 in 1980 and 82,169 in 1990, so most of its homes are between 35 and 55 years old. This page explains what that means for leaks, flooding and mold, and how our 24/7 emergency line serves the 75006, 75007 and 75010 ZIP codes.

Carrollton at a glance

Carrollton lies in parts of Dallas, Denton and Collin counties. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for all of it.

What a 1970s to 1990 housing stock means for water damage

Carrollton's population grew by 193 percent between 1970 and 1980 alone, and a house built then is now more than 45 years old. At that age the original supply lines, shutoff valves, water heater connections and tub drains are the parts most likely to fail. Pipe fittings that have corroded behind a wall can leak slowly for weeks before a stain shows up on the other side.

If you own an older Carrollton home, a plumber's inspection of the visible supply lines and shutoffs, plus replacing rubber washing machine hoses with braided ones, is inexpensive protection. When a leak does appear, water mitigation starts with moisture readings to find how far the water traveled inside the walls and floors.

When water is spreading in Carrollton

Treat standing water, a dripping ceiling or any leak that you cannot stop as an emergency. Call the 24/7 line before you start cleanup, and let the dispatcher tell you what to move and what to leave. Same-day response is the goal whenever it is available, and no arrival time is promised.

For standing water, emergency water removal uses pumps and extraction units to get the water out, and drying equipment follows. For storm water or a sewer backup, the job is treated as flood damage restoration because the water is contaminated.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop the source

Close the main water valve or the fixture valve. If the source is a roof leak, place a bucket and move furniture away from the drip.

Protect people and power

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Keep children and pets out of contaminated water.

Lift and photograph

Move valuables off the floor and take photos of everything before it is moved. Then call (817) 623-9267.

ZIP codes we cover in Carrollton

Carrollton has three standard ZIP codes. ZIP 75007 is the largest by population, with 53,960 residents counted in the 2020 Census, and 75006 follows at 47,811. ZIP 75010 is mostly in Denton County and was counted at 33,186. Crews respond across all three, and each is covered by the same emergency line.

The county line matters for paperwork. Because Carrollton spans three counties, the city, county and utility details can differ by address, so ask the City of Carrollton building inspection office what applies to your property before a permit-sized repair begins.

Mold after a Carrollton leak

An older house that has had a slow leak behind a bathroom wall or around a water heater can develop mold in the cavity. The Texas Department of Insurance notes that mold can start growing within a day after a leak, so fast drying is the best protection. If growth is already visible, mold remediation covers inspection, containment and removal under the S520 industry standard.

Restore

Once readings are dry, the house is repaired back to its pre-loss condition.

The sequence matters. Skipping straight to repair while framing is still wet is how stains, swelling and mold come back, and this is why daily moisture readings drive the schedule. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the published industry standard for professional water damage restoration, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it.

Working With Your Insurance Claim

Documentation your adjuster will ask for

We photograph the damage, record moisture readings and keep an equipment log, so you have a written record of what happened and what was done. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowner and renter policies, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Local knowledge

Water Damage Risks Specific to Coppell

Coppell grew quickly between 1980 and 2000, rising from 3,826 residents to 35,958, and nearly all residentially zoned land was developed by 2000. Many homes are now 25 to 45 years old. Original supply lines, water heaters, washing machine hoses and roof flashing have aged with them, which is why hidden leaks and slow failures are a steady source of calls.

Four waterways flow through or border the city: Denton Creek, Grapevine Creek, Cottonwood Creek and the Elm Fork of the Trinity River. The city's Engineering Department keeps floodplain maps and can tell you whether your address is in or near a floodplain, and a separate flood policy is the only kind that covers rising water.

During the June 2015 flooding from Tropical Storm Bill, hundreds of residents woke to Denton Creek in their yards, so check the floodplain status of your address before the next heavy rain.

Commercial water losses are common too. South Belt Line Road, Freeport Parkway, Lakeshore Drive and Fritz Drive hold business center, office and commerce center buildings where one failed line can affect several suites. Tenants should call right away and notify building management.

Freezes are less frequent here than farther north, but the National Weather Service counts an average of 29 per season at Dallas/Fort Worth, enough to burst exposed pipes in garages, attics and exterior walls. Cabinet doors open on exterior walls and a known main shutoff are the cheap protections.

Common questions

Water Damage Questions We Hear in Coppell

Do you respond to water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Coppell?

Yes. The emergency line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day response is the goal whenever it is available, and the dispatcher will give you shutoff and safety steps while a crew is arranged. No fixed arrival time is promised.

How soon do I need to call after a leak?

As soon as you see it. The Texas Department of Insurance notes that mold can start growing within a day after a leak, and wet drywall, pad and insulation get harder to save with every hour.

What will the first visit involve?

A crew checks safety, finds the water's path with moisture meters and a thermal camera, and explains the scope and how it will be priced before work begins. Extraction and drying equipment can start the same visit when the scope is clear.

Does restoration include waterproofing a basement or foundation?

No. Restoration repairs damage after water gets in. Waterproofing or foundation work stops water from entering, and that is a different trade. We can tell you what we see so you know which specialist to call.

Do I need to call my insurance company before you start?

Many policies expect prompt notice, and emergency steps to protect the property are usually expected as well. Call your insurer, photograph the damage and keep receipts. The dispatcher can tell you what records we keep.

Which towns do you serve besides Coppell?

Carrollton, Irving, Grapevine and Lewisville, including ZIP codes such as 75007, 75062, 76051 and 75067. Each has its own page with local details.

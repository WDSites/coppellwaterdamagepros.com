Las Colinas, Valley Ranch and Cypress Waters

Las Colinas is a planned community of more than 12,000 acres, with offices, hotels and a convention center that opened in 2011. Valley Ranch is a master-planned neighborhood that was the Dallas Cowboys' headquarters from 1985 to 2016, and Cypress Waters is another area we cover. Larger and multi-unit buildings have long supply runs, shared walls and mechanical rooms, so a single failure can reach several units or floors.

In multi-unit and commercial buildings, call the 24/7 line and notify building management at the same time. A restoration crew can run water mitigation across several suites while management closes the affected valve.

When water is spreading in Irving

Treat any leak you cannot stop, any water in more than one room and any backup from a toilet or floor drain as an emergency. Wide, flat, dense neighborhoods mean that a washing machine or water heater failure in one home may also reach a garage or a neighbor's wall.

Standing water goes first, through emergency water removal. If the water entered from outside during a storm, flood damage restoration applies, since runoff is contaminated.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Shut off the water

Close the main valve or the valve behind the fixture. In a multi-unit building, tell management so the riser can be closed.

Make the room safe

Cut power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry to reach. Keep people out of any water that may be a sewage backup.

Document, then call

Photograph the damage and lift valuables. Then call (817) 623-9267 for the 24/7 line.

Irving's water supply and where your responsibility begins

The City of Irving states that most of its water comes from Lake Chapman, is pumped to Lewisville Lake and is treated by the City of Dallas for Irving, with some water bought directly from Dallas. When a problem is in the street or at the meter, it belongs to the city's water utility. When water is inside the house, it belongs to you and your insurer, and a restoration crew can start drying right away.

If you notice discolored water or low pressure on your whole street, call the city. If you see water on your own floor, call us.

Vacant units and leaks that run unseen

The Census counted 6.1 percent of Irving's housing units as vacant in 2020. A leak in an empty apartment, rental or second home can run for days before anyone notices, and the resulting damage is far larger than the same leak in an occupied home. Property managers and owners should ask for periodic checks, especially after storms.

Heavy spring storms matter too. May is Irving's wettest month on average, so roof flashing, gutters and window seals deserve a look before then. A ceiling stain after a May storm should be checked with a moisture meter before it is painted.

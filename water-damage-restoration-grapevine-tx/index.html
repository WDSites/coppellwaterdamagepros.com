Water damage restoration in Grapevine, TX often starts with weather. The city sits next to Grapevine Lake, which the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers impounded in 1952, and heavy North Texas rain can push the lake and the creeks below it in ways that reach homes. Our 24/7 emergency line serves ZIP 76051 and the surrounding area, and this page covers what to do when water arrives from the sky, the lake or your own plumbing.

Grapevine at a glance

Lake and creek country

Grapevine is mostly in Tarrant County with small parts in Dallas and Denton counties, and the lake and creeks shape its storm risk.

Emergency dispatch around the clock

The line is answered 24/7. Same-day response is the goal when available, with no arrival time promised.

A higher address than Coppell

Grapevine's elevation is about 630 feet, compared with 512 feet in Coppell. Higher ground does not make a home immune from roof and runoff damage.

What the June 2015 lake rise taught Grapevine homeowners

In June 2015, rain from Tropical Depression Bill raised Grapevine Lake more than a foot, and the Army Corps forecast twice the amount of water going over the spillway. About 800 apartments in northeast Grapevine were at risk of being cut off by floodwater, and TxDOT closed FM 2499 between Grapevine and Flower Mound.

The practical lesson is access. During a lake rise, getting a restoration crew or a plumber to a property can be slow, so deal with the water you can control early. Move valuables up, shut off power to affected rooms if it is safe and call the 24/7 line as soon as water enters.

When water is spreading in Grapevine

Water entering from outside is contaminated, and the job is treated as flood damage restoration. Water from a broken supply line, water heater or appliance is usually cleaner, and water mitigation begins with extraction and drying. Either way, call before cleanup starts so the crew knows what to expect.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop what you can

Close the main water valve if the source is plumbing. For storm water, move to higher ground and avoid wading through it.

Power and safety

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel dry. Call 911 or the utility for any electrical or gas concern.

Lift, photograph, call

Move valuables up, photograph the damage and call (817) 623-9267 for the 24/7 line.

Historic Main Street, restaurants and hotels

Grapevine's Historic Main Street holds shops, restaurants, theaters and city offices, and the city is home to the Grapevine Mills outlet center and hotels near the DFW Airport north entrance. Commercial kitchens, dishwashers, ice machines and restroom lines are common sources of water losses in these buildings.

Business owners should call the 24/7 line and tell building management at the same time. A failed fixture on one level can send water through the ceiling below, so the job often covers more than one space.

Where Grapevine's water comes from

According to the city's water quality report, Grapevine uses surface water from Lake Grapevine and also purchases water from the Trinity River Authority. The city's water utility can be reached at 817-410-3330 for problems in the street or at the meter. Water inside your house, including a failed line past the meter, is handled by you and your insurer, and a restoration crew can start drying immediately.

Safety before restoration starts

Electricity and standing water

Do not stand in water to touch a breaker, an outlet or an appliance. If water is reaching the panel, stay out of the room and call 911 or your electric utility. Keep children and pets out of water that may be contaminated.

Close the main water valve if you can reach it safely. Move small valuables to a dry room, and photograph what you see when it is safe. If a gas smell is present, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility.

How drying works after the water is out

A crew removes standing water, then uses moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to find wet material behind walls and under floors. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings are repeated until the materials are dry. Our work uses ANSI/IICRC S500 as a reference.

See structural drying and dehumidification for the equipment and the daily checks. Materials that cannot be dried safely may have to be removed, and we explain which before work begins.

Insurance documentation

Whether a loss is covered depends on its cause and your policy. Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope can help describe the damage, and your insurer decides what applies. Call your insurer promptly and ask about deductibles and flood coverage, since rising outside water is usually handled separately from a plumbing failure.

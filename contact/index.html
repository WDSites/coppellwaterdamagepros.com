Be ready

What to Have Ready When You Call

  • The address of the property and a callback number.
  • Where the water is coming from, if you can see it, and whether it is still flowing.
  • Which rooms or floors are wet and about how large the area is.
  • Whether water has reached outlets, appliances or the electrical panel.
  • Your insurance company name, in case the dispatcher asks, and photos if you have taken any.

If you cannot answer everything, call anyway. The dispatcher will ask the right questions and will not hold up safety steps while you look for information.

What comes next

What the Dispatcher Will Do

The dispatcher will ask a few short questions, give you safety steps first and then arrange a crew. If the situation is not an emergency, such as old mold or a stain that stopped growing, they will explain what an inspection involves and how scope and price are set.

For insurance, photograph the damage, keep damaged items until your adjuster has seen them and save receipts. The Texas Department of Insurance advises documenting everything before repairs begin.
